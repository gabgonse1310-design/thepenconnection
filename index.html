Good to know

A few practical details.

How much does a project cost?

Quotes depend on the service, length, complexity, and deadline. Send a short brief and, if you have one, a sample of your text to discuss the scope and receive an individual quote.

What is the difference between proofreading and copywriting?

Proofreading checks an existing text for errors and consistency. Copywriting creates text for a specific audience and purpose. If your draft needs substantial restructuring or rewriting, we’ll discuss that separately before agreeing on the work.

Can you work to my style guide?

Include your preferred English spelling convention, style guide, and any brand guidelines in your brief so the requirements can be agreed before work begins.

What should I include in my enquiry?

The service you need, the type of text, your audience, approximate word count, and desired deadline. Please avoid including sensitive material in your initial enquiry; we can agree how to share documents next.
